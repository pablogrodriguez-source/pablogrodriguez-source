# Pablo Rodríguez · Product Designer
Senior Motion Designer → Product Designer · AI-native · Buenos Aires, remote.

For ten years my job was making things understandable: a bank's green car loan, a local-produce app, a video platform, a hemp supply chain, a college that grew from 37 learners to 8,155. I learned how products work by explaining them in motion, for teams at Nightingale College, IB5k, Soapboxx, Aptly, Coderhouse and Deloitte Dutch Caribbean. Since October 2025 I design them myself, with large language models.
